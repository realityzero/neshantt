The formula

Keep everything big and neutral: navy jacket, grey or cream knit, charcoal trousers. Put all the colour in a beanie or a scarf, and swap it each morning. Three cheap accessories give you twenty-three different photos.

  1. Look at what is behind you. Water, snow, fog and pine are cool: wear warm. Red walls, red leaves and gold roofs are loud: wear quiet.
  2. Never match the backdrop. Red at the palace, green in the bamboo, mustard under the ginkgo: you disappear.
  3. One accent at a time. A red beanie and a mustard scarf together fight each other on camera.
  4. Dress for the coldest hour, not the average. Sunrise waits, boat decks and ridge wind are where you get cold.

Five outfits

Every day uses one of these layer stacks. The letter on each day card tells you which.

C City

9 to 15 °C 7 days

  • BaseT-shirt (thermal top on a grey day)
  • MidSweatshirt or sweater
  • OuterDown jacket, open, or carried
  • LegsTrousers or jeans
  • FeetSneakers

You will warm up on stairs and cool down in unheated cafés. Keep the sweater on indoors.

M Mountain

0 to 10 °C 8 days

  • BaseThermal top + thermal leggings
  • MidSweater or fleece
  • OuterDown jacket, zipped; shell over it in spray or drizzle
  • LegsHiking pants
  • FeetWaterproof boots, thermal socks
  • ExtrasBeanie, liner gloves, buff

Peel the down jacket on long climbs, put it back on the second you stop.

D Deep cold

−10 to 2 °C 7 days

  • BaseThermals, top and bottom, all day
  • MidThickest sweater
  • OuterDown jacket, hood up in wind
  • LegsTrousers over thermal leggings
  • FeetBoots + thermal socks; 冰爪 microspikes on ice
  • ExtrasOuter gloves over liners, scarf, 暖宝宝 in pockets

Wind is the enemy, not the number. Cover neck, ears and wrists first.

V Via ferrata

2 to 10 °C 1 day

  • BaseThermal top
  • MidThin fleece
  • OuterBright softshell or fleece, no down
  • LegsStretchy hiking pants, never jeans
  • FeetGrippy boots or approach shoes
  • ExtrasGrip gloves, fitted beanie under the helmet, nothing dangling

Down jackets tear on rock and get in the way of the harness. Bring a bright, close-fitting layer.

S Snow

−18 to −8 °C 2 days

  • BaseThermals, synthetic or wool, never cotton
  • MidFleece
  • OuterRented snow suit 雪服
  • HeadHelmet + goggles + buff
  • HandsWaterproof gloves
  • Pads护具: padded shorts and knee pads, wrist guards

Snowboarding beginners fall onto their backside and wrists. Rent the pads; you will thank yourself.
