Set these up before any window opens

Nearly every booking below is real-name and checked against your passport. Getting the accounts verified now means you're not doing it at 20:00 on the night the Forbidden City opens.

12306, verified with your passport

The only official seller of train tickets. Register this week with the passport: automatic checks of foreign passports often fail and manual review has taken 3–5 working days. The first window is Mon 9 Nov 08:45.

  • Sales open 14 days before travel ("15 days including today") at each boarding station's own hour (成都东 08:45, 重庆北/东 11:00, 清河 16:00…).
  • Do the face check (人证核验) in the Chinese interface now: the waitlist (候补) needs it.
  • Seats are held 30 min for payment; pay via Alipay/WeChat with your card linked.
  • Refunds free until 8 days out.

WeChat and Alipay, both verified

Link your Visa/Mastercard in WeChat Pay and Alipay and finish identity verification with the passport. Most booking mini-programs live in WeChat; Leshan's 大佛旅游 and Jiuzhaigou's platform also run inside Alipay, which tends to accept passports more readily.

Check that SMS codes reach your +65 number: some forms only take +86, so have a fallback (Trip.com, the hotel, the window).

Trip.com with the passport saved

Add yourself as a traveller with the passport name exactly as printed. Trip.com is the official foreigner route for the Panda Base, Du Fu's cottage and the Grand Canyon, and the fallback for most other cards here. Turn on notifications: refunds and slot changes arrive there.

Insurance that covers skiing and altitude

Thaiwoo's lift ticket includes only basic cover, and Alipay's ski-insurance products mostly bar foreign nationals. Make sure your Singapore travel policy covers recreational skiing, the via ferrata and altitude to ~3,100 m (Long Lake at Jiuzhaigou, the Golden Summit).

The hotels with thin supply

Your call, but these are the ones that can run out: Zhangzha (Jiuzhaigou, 23–24 Nov), the Golden Summit (27 Nov), Dingxiangrong (5 Dec) and Thaiwoo village (9–10 Dec). Book them with free cancellation now; everything else can wait.

7 · How to

Finding the accounts, and what to type

Find the right account in WeChat

  • Tap the search bar at the top of the Chats tab and paste the Chinese name from a card. Switch the results tab to Official Accounts (公众号) or Mini Programs (小程序).
  • The real one has the institution's logo and, under its profile, the operating organisation (账号主体), e.g. a museum or a 景区管理 company. Booking lives in the bottom menu: look for 购票, 预约, 门票 or 个人预约.
  • Faster: scan a QR code on this page with WeChat's scanner (＋ → Scan). Every QR here comes from WeChat's own server for that account ID, and its logo was checked by eye.

Avoid the look-alikes

Search results are full of reseller and scam accounts with names like 预约参观入口, 博物馆预约入馆, 旅游景点预约入口, 科技馆博物院馆预约, 长江索道预约门票, 北京预约慕田峪, 北京景区门票预约 or 北京景点预约票. They charge "service fees" or harvest passport numbers. Free museums are never sold. If an account isn't run by the venue itself, close it; use the venue, Trip.com, or the window.

Typing your passport in

  • Document type: pick 护照 (passport), sometimes listed as 外国人护照 or under 其他证件. Never 身份证, which is the Chinese ID card.
  • Name: exactly as on the passport's data page, in capitals. Most forms take surname then given names; if one rejects spaces, run them together.
  • Nationality 新加坡 (Singapore). Phone: try +65 first; if SMS never arrives, the form only takes +86 and you need a fallback.

When a mini-program refuses your passport

  1. Look for an English or "foreigner" entrance in the same account (故宫, 国博, 12306 and Trip.com have them).
  2. Trip.com: same inventory for most sights, passport accepted, small fee at most.
  3. Ask the hotel front desk to book under your passport; most will.
  4. The ticket window with your passport. Most sights keep window or on-site booking for foreigners, but the Forbidden City, the National Museum and Lama Temple do not sell at the gate.

Reading Chinese-only screens

  • Inside WeChat, long-press any article → Translate. For mini-programs, screenshot and use iPhone Live Text or Google Lens to translate.
  • Keywords to spot: 预约 book · 购票 buy ticket · 售罄 sold out · 已约满 fully booked · 候补 waitlist · 退票 refund · 改签 change · 实名 real-name · 场次 / 时段 time slot · 换票 exchange ticket.

Night-of and day-of habits

  • Book the night before for weather-dependent things (Tianmen, the glass bridge, the via ferrata, Wushan): fog and ice closures are announced on the venue account by about 07:30.
  • Screenshot every booking QR. Mountain signal is patchy and the gate scanner doesn't care where the image came from.
  • Carry the physical passport, not a photo. Real-name gates check it by hand for foreigners.
